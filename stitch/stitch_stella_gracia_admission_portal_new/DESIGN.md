## Brand & Style

The design system is engineered to project **academic prestige, reliability, and modern efficiency**. It serves as the digital front door for a school admission system (SPMB), where the primary goal is to instill confidence in parents and students through a structured and transparent experience.

The visual style is **Corporate Modern with a Minimalist lens**. We utilize significant whitespace to reduce cognitive load during complex application processes. The aesthetic is defined by high-quality typography, a structured grid, and subtle depth to guide the user through sequential tasks without overwhelming them. It avoids unnecessary decoration, focusing instead on clarity and the authoritative presence of the institution.

**Key Brand Attributes:**
- **Prestigious:** Reflecting high educational standards.
- **Trustworthy:** Ensuring data security and process integrity.
- **Efficient:** Streamlining the path from inquiry to enrollment.
- **Accessible:** Ensuring information is legible and the navigation is intuitive.

## Colors

The palette is anchored by **Deep Navy (#243070)** to establish a foundation of stability and authority. **Royal Blue (#3c50bc)** is used for primary actions and interactive states to provide a more vibrant, modern energy.

The secondary **Gold/Tan (#bd9c4f)** and **Light Gold (#ffd26a)** tones are used sparingly as accents—specifically for high-importance labels, achievements, or "Call to Action" highlights that need to feel premium. 

For the background, we use a very soft neutral gray to reduce screen glare, maintaining a "Clean/Light" aesthetic while differentiating surfaces from pure white cards.

## Typography

This design system uses a dual-font strategy. **Poppins** provides a geometric, friendly yet professional structure for headlines, making the school's brand feel modern and approachable. **Inter** is utilized for all body copy and UI labels due to its exceptional legibility at small sizes, which is critical for long application forms.

- **Headlines:** Use Bold or Semi-Bold weights to create a clear visual hierarchy.
- **Body:** Stick to Regular weights for readability, using Medium for emphasis within text blocks.
- **Labels:** Use Medium/Semi-Bold weights for form labels and button text to ensure they stand out as interactive elements.

## Layout & Spacing

We employ a **12-column fluid grid system** for desktop, transitioning to a 4-column system for mobile. The layout philosophy is centered on **generous margins and breathing room** to prevent the "data-heavy" feeling often associated with school systems.

- **Safe Margins:** Use 24px (md) for mobile edges and 48px+ (lg) for desktop edges.
- **Vertical Rhythm:** Content blocks should be separated by 48px (lg) to create a clear distinction between sections.
- **Form Layouts:** Input fields should span 6 columns on desktop (half width) or 12 columns for long text to maintain a comfortable reading eye-track.

## Elevation & Depth

To maintain a "Professional & Clean" look, we use **Tonal Layering** supplemented by **Ambient Shadows**. This design system avoids heavy shadows, opting instead for thin, high-clarity outlines or extremely soft blurs to lift active elements.

- **Level 0 (Base):** Neutral background (#f8f9fa).
- **Level 1 (Cards):** Pure white surface (#ffffff) with a 1px border (#e9ecef) or a very subtle shadow (0px 4px 20px rgba(0,0,0,0.05)).
- **Level 2 (Modals/Dropdowns):** Pure white surface with a more pronounced shadow to indicate focus (0px 10px 30px rgba(36,48,112,0.12)), using a hint of the primary dark blue in the shadow tint.

## Shapes

The design system uses a **Rounded** language (8px base radius). This strikes a balance between the "sharpness" of traditional institutional systems and the "softness" of consumer apps. 

- **Standard Elements:** Buttons, inputs, and small cards use the base 8px (rounded) radius.
- **Large Containers:** Section containers or main application cards may use 16px (rounded-lg) for a softer, more modern aesthetic.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background #243070, Text White, 8px radius. High-contrast for the main "Submit" or "Apply" actions.
- **Secondary:** Border 1px #3c50bc, Text #3c50bc, Background Transparent. For "Back" or "Save Draft".
- **Accent:** Background #bd9c4f, Text White. Reserved for "Scholarship Info" or "Special Programs".

### Input Fields
- **Default:** White background, 1px border (#dee2e6), 8px radius.
- **Focus State:** 2px border #3c50bc with a soft outer glow.
- **Labels:** Positioned above the field in Inter Semi-Bold (Label-md).

### Progress Bars
- **Track:** #e9ecef (Light Gray).
- **Fill:** Gradient from #3c50bc to #243070.
- **Usage:** Essential for showing application completion percentage (e.g., "Step 2 of 5").

### Data Visualization Cards
- Used for the applicant dashboard.
- Features large display numbers (e.g., "Days until deadline") in Poppins Bold.
- Background: White with a subtle left-hand border accent in #bd9c4f (Gold) to denote importance.

### Chips & Tags
- Used for application status (e.g., "Pending", "Verified", "Action Required").
- Use low-saturation background tints of the status colors (Success/Error) with high-saturation text for readability.
